# Mastery Rubric

Use this rubric at the end of each learning day.

| Level | Explanation | Mathematics | Implementation | Evidence and judgment |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 — Not yet | Cannot state the mechanism accurately. | Cannot identify the relevant quantities. | Cannot locate or run the mechanism. | Treats outputs as proof without qualification. |
| 1 — Recognize | Describes the basic purpose with help. | Recognizes the main symbols. | Runs an existing example with help. | Records results but mixes observation and interpretation. |
| 2 — Explain | Explains the mechanism and main trade-offs. | Follows and checks a derivation. | Modifies a readable implementation. | States a hypothesis, controls, and limitations. |
| 3 — Build | Explains tensor flow and failure modes precisely. | Reconstructs the derivation and verifies it numerically. | Implements and tests the mechanism independently. | Designs a controlled comparison and defends the conclusion. |
| 4 — Transfer | Teaches the mechanism and compares alternatives. | Adapts the derivation to a new setting. | Integrates it into a larger training system safely. | Predicts failures, resolves conflicting evidence, and proposes the next experiment. |

## Day 1 target

Reach at least Level 2 for laboratory practice:

- identify an experiment from code, environment, hardware, model/data revisions,
  configuration, and seed;
- distinguish observations, supported interpretations, and unsupported claims;
- write a falsifiable hypothesis and controlled comparison;
- explain the three non-additive DGX Spark memory views;
- reproduce and critically interpret one Qwen3-0.6B smoke test.
