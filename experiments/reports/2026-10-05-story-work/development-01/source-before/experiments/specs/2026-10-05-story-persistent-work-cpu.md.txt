## Actual fresh-process replay and failure suffix

For all four seed/mode arms, run an uninterrupted actual five-update reference
and retain every numerical record. Independently stop the same-source accounted
Session after update2, durably save its original completed state and work receipt.
After that boundary inject an admitted failure on the second accumulation loss
call, retaining any first-microbatch work and the permanent whole-update
reservation. The accounted Session must be poisoned: no new update, observation
or publication of a partially mutated state. This failure is not a committed
successful-target presentation.

Close and reopen the same physical journal using the independent saved receipt,
preserving its later failure suffix. In a fresh subprocess, construct the exact
same original Session under an explicitly opened allowance, restore the old
completed boundary, and continue through update5. Require exact next selected
IDs, full successful records/tail, parameters/Adam, stream/global RNG and target/

Additional separately identified evaluation/activation failures test retained
panel reservations. They do not replace the four actual fresh replay arms.
Restore/save attempts count their declared operation, not generic byte, finite
tree, deserializer, cloning or physical storage work. Unaccounted teaching calls
and legacy checkpoint compatibility remain explicit.
