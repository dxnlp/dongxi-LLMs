## Deadline and shutdown controls

The controller's clock does not call the memory probe or fsynced logger.
Separate owned helpers communicate through bounded nonblocking datagrams;
malformed or oversized responses refuse rather than stall frame assembly.
Failed, stale or incomplete observations stop only the owned invocation.
Sanitized conflict inspection supplies refusal evidence, never signal targets.

The leader remains unreaped until unconditional owned-session TERM/KILL drain,
including a natural exit0 with a remaining worker. Observed escaped descendants
use retained PID/create-time handles. Denied TERM does not skip KILL/reap.
Helper and queue-feeder cleanup is bounded and any live/unknown outcome remains
visible. The isolated controller rejects arbitrary threaded callers. Late
cleanup/fallback helpers use spawn rather than forking after feeder threads start.

A stored result cannot attest its own future fsync acknowledgment. Its payload
labels that acknowledgment unknown-at-write; the returned receipt records the
separate observed writer ACK. Failed logging retains available partial files
and a bounded fallback attempt, not an invented durable success.
