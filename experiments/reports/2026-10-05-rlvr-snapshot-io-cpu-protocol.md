## Refusal and source-order panel

- Exhausted inspect/load/save refuses before payload hashing/deserialization or
  expensive shared tree/finite/clone/serialization work. Model-semantic validation
  and caller capture are separate; omitted or mismatched required hook refuses
  even before those caller operations and lifecycle output creation.
- Pending/completed schema, cursor beyond fixed horizon and pending at terminal
  horizon refuse before journals, inspection or model providers. Valid pending
  below horizon remains supported. Changed declared bytes/hash/science,
  missing/extra/boolean caps, changed/copy journal/receipt and v1 payload in an
  explicitly accounted contract refuse without migration or refills.
- Preserve actual partial serializer/publication failure, prior committed
  payload/receipt and permanent I/O/model spending. Observer/lifecycle failures
  remain separately identified rather than claimed as production incidents.
- Actual CLI ordering controls use native parsing/open/inspection/identity
  routes with explicit mocked platform/parent/interface providers and a model
  loader sentinel. Spy both generic identity passes and payload verifier;
  exhausted admission cannot secretly hash the payload or allocate/load models.
  A successful inspection records measured header separately from expectations
  before the sentinel. Bootstrap FIFO/duplicate/oversize/change controls are
  bounded metadata refusals, not authenticated supplier evidence.
