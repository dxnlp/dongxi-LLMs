## Observations

- The process exited with status 0 after reporting all three requested optimizer
  steps.
- All three reported loss values were finite.
- Loss was not monotonic: it decreased at step 2 and increased at step 3.
- Minimum whole-system `MemAvailable` was 109.74 GiB, above the precommitted
  24 GiB reserve.
- The expected model and tokenizer artifacts were written.
- The trainer did not report gradient norms, so no numerical gradient-norm claim
  is made.

## Interpretations

- The hypothesis and all precommitted success criteria were supported.
- On this recorded host and stack, this exact profile can load and preprocess the
  selected data, load Qwen3-0.6B, execute BF16 SDPA full-SFT forward/backward and
  optimizer work for three steps, save the resulting model, and preserve the
  required system-memory headroom.
- The non-monotonic three-step loss is not a failure: the specification required
  finite losses, and different microbatches can have different difficulty.

## Claims not established

- The run does not demonstrate general capability improvement.
- It does not establish convergence or long-run stability.
- It does not establish exact numerical reproducibility on another host.
- It does not establish safety for larger batches or sequence lengths.
- It does not show that this recipe or attention backend is optimal.

## Failures or surprises

- No execution or memory-safety failure occurred.
- PyTorch emitted a capability-range warning for `sm_121`; the actual SDPA,
  backward, optimizer, and save path nevertheless executed successfully. The
  warning remains evidence to retain rather than suppress.
- The compatibility trainer is deprecated upstream. It remains the validated
  fallback here because the current packed OLMo-core SFT path requires an
  intra-document mask unsupported by its Torch/SDPA backend on this setup.
