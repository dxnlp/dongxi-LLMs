## Tension to discuss

The training process can stay within its update count and still exceed its
allowance during baseline generation, reference scoring, final evaluation,
recovery retries or checkpoint publication. Ask what is missing from a displayed
“100/100 updates complete” before showing a solution. Use an argument, not a
calculation quiz.

## The two clocks that must not rewind together

The numerical snapshot may end at update3. A separately retained resource journal
may already contain a reservation for an attempted update4. Restoring3 recovers
the numerical state; it must not erase the later resource spending. Validate
the snapshot's trusted journal prefix, replay later retained entries, then charge
the retry before starting it. Conservative capacity and known actual work are
different measurements. Unknown failed-call work stays unknown, not zero.

The DPO path makes this concrete: chosen/rejected responses are scored under
both current policy and original reference. A single-branch length geometry
omits three branches, and successful logical input counts still exclude backward
recomputation. Generation may consume an entire growing prefix on each step.
Changing accumulation, skipping data or truncating output to fit the final
allowance changes the objective or evaluation and needs a different contract.

## Storage is a publication process

Old snapshot, new partial payload, temporary marker and published marker can
coexist. Reserve the bundle before creating any member. Failures can retain
partial files and consumed entries. An exclusive cooperative writer protects only
its own writes; arbitrary exports and children need a separately tested physical
quota. Do not remove old model files to make a save fit without explicit authority.

## A retained receipt must come before the expensive read

Worked answers31–32 extend the same argument. Two successful loads yield identical
tensors; a rejected third load still reads bytes and executes generic checks.
The journal retains three reservations after reopening from the older receipt,
and refuses a fourth attempt before work. Its external receipt names the exact
payload/scientific/I/O contracts and pre-save prefix; later save completion cannot
be serialized inside its own fixed payload. A generic identity hash performed
before admission would bypass the reader guard, so bind bounded metadata first
and establish actual payload identity only in the admitted path.
